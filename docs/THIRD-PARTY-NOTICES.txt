CAK format research acknowledgment
==================================

The independent FDIR inspector was written using Nenkai's Bakery/CakeTool
format research: header/version flags, section layout, file/directory entries,
lookup indices, chunk bounds and string layout. No Bakery binaries, game
assets, decryption keys or third-party example archives are distributed here.

Source: https://github.com/Nenkai/Bakery
Research revision: f704d3724d713f36cff74a82e7466d5710b510de
Relevant files: CakeRegistryFile.cs, CakeFileEntry.cs, CakeDirInfo.cs,
CakeEntryLookup.cs, AbstractVersionableCakeEntity.cs and CakeFileBuilder.cs.
